Body Scrub

Body scrub appears in the Flower Mound and Southlake Fresha service categories. The original Phuket Signature Package describes a 60-minute scrub using a choice of sea salt or sugar before the massage portion of the package. Exact body scrub options may differ between packages and stand-alone appointments.

Sauna

Thaissage Center's original Home describes an optional 30-minute sauna after a massage and identifies Flower Mound as its location. The newer Fresha listings also identify sauna at Southlake, creating a difference between sources. Check the relevant location's current sauna menu and booking requirements before visiting.

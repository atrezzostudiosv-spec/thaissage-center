Where Techniques Meet

Thaissage Center describes Thai Combination as starting with Thai-style pressure and stretching and continuing with Swedish massage techniques such as gliding, kneading, and rubbing. It offers a way to explore both styles without choosing just one.

THAISSAGE / 02

THE BLEND / 02

For Guests Who Want Variety

Thai Combination can suit someone who enjoys the movement-focused character of Thai massage but also wants the familiar rhythm of classic Swedish bodywork. The details of the treatment and the level of pressure should be discussed with the provider at your appointment.

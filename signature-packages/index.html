Named for three places in Thailand, these experiences combine massage and spa treatments into longer visits. Explore the sequences below, then confirm current durations, prices, and availability with your preferred Thaissage Center location.

THAISSAGE / 01

TIME, BEAUTIFULLY SPENT / 01

Chiang Mai Signature Package

Menu duration: 120 minutes — confirm when booking

A Thai-centered sequence with two parts:

  • 45 minutes — Traditional Thai Massage: pressure-based bodywork and stretching.
  • 75 minutes — Thai Herbal Compress Ball: a warmed herbal compress treatment that complements the earlier session.

A signature experience rooted in Thai-style bodywork, moving from assisted stretching and pressure techniques into a warm herbal-compress session.

Bangkok Signature Package

Menu duration: 150 minutes — confirm when booking

A three-part spa sequence:

  • 30 minutes — Sauna
  • 45 minutes — Body Scrub
  • 75 minutes — Aromatherapy Oil Massage

A longer spa visit designed around the transition from sauna to body scrub and an aromatherapy oil massage. Check the selected location for current sauna availability and package details.

TIME, BEAUTIFULLY SPENT / 03

Phuket Signature Package

Menu duration: 120 minutes — confirm when booking

A two-part body-care and massage sequence:

  • 60 minutes — Body Scrub: the published description lists sea salt or sugar as options.
  • 60 minutes — Coconut Oil Massage: choose from a Swedish or Deep Tissue style according to the published description.

A body scrub followed by a coconut-oil massage, pairing exfoliation-focused body care with a classic massage-style finish.

Are the packages the same length?

No. The published package descriptions list Chiang Mai and Phuket at 120 minutes, and Bangkok at 150 minutes. Confirm current durations when booking.

Can I assume all three packages are available in Frisco?

No. The Frisco menu reviewed does not list a Signature Combo Packages category; confirm directly with the location.

Where can I find current package prices?

Use the location's booking menu or call that location. Current package prices may vary. Check the selected location's Fresha menu for current pricing before booking.
